Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to our most common customer inquiries regarding ordering, sizing, shipping, and authentic surplus sourcing.

Ordering & Payment

Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?

Yes, Cash on Delivery is available nationwide across all major cities and towns in Pakistan. You pay in cash when the courier rider delivers your package.

Can I pay via debit/credit card or bank transfer?

Yes, we accept Visa, Mastercard, direct online banking transfers, EasyPaisa, and JazzCash at checkout.

Shipping & Delivery

How long will my delivery take?

Karachi deliveries take 1–2 business days. Other major urban hubs take 2–3 business days, and outer regions take 3–5 business days via TCS, Leopards, or Trax.

How can I track my order?

Once dispatched, you will receive an automatic SMS and email with your courier tracking link and tracking ID to monitor delivery status live.

How do I qualify for Free Shipping?

All orders with a subtotal exceeding PKR 4,999 automatically qualify for free shipping at checkout.

Products & Exchanges

What does "Export Surplus" mean?

Export surplus garments are genuine overruns, cancelled international shipments, or wholesale factory surpluses produced in Pakistan for international markets. They feature premium fabrics and international build standards at factory-direct pricing.

Can I exchange my item if the size doesn't fit?

Absolutely. We offer a 7-day exchange window. Contact our support team on WhatsApp or email with your Order ID, and we will arrange a replacement size.
