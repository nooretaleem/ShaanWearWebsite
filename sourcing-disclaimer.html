About Our Stock & Sourcing Disclaimer

Our mission is simple: bringing export-grade luxury garments to Pakistani consumers at unbeatable value through transparent sourcing.

Genuine Export Surplus & Leftovers

At SHAANWEAR, we curate genuine export leftovers, overstock, and factory surplus garments sourced directly from high-end apparel export production facilities across Pakistan. These pieces are crafted using internationally certified fabrics, precision stitching, and premium hardware engineered for export across Europe, North America, and the Middle East.

Official Distributor Notice

Please note that while all items featured in our surplus sections are authentic quality products sourced from legitimate wholesale export production channels, SHAANWEAR is an independent retailer and is not an official distributor, authorized representative, or licensed franchise for third-party brands featured in our surplus collection. All third-party trademarks, brand names, and logos belong solely to their respective registered owners.

Strict Multi-Tier Quality Inspection

Every garment arriving at our Karachi inspection facility undergoes individual hand-checking for fabric integrity, color-fastness, seam strength, zipper durability, and sizing accuracy before being approved for dispatch.

Unmatched Value Proposition

By eliminating overseas shipping markups, middleman agency fees, and international retail overheads, we pass direct wholesale savings onto our customers. You receive authentic export-grade streetwear and essentials at a fraction of high-street retail costs.
